# Links & Citations

Where the formulas and reference tables in this project come from. Feng shui sources disagree with
each other often; where they did, we cross-checked at least two and noted the disagreement in the
relevant primer. This is also the honest record that the numbers weren't invented.

## A note on one rejected source
- A 24-mountains table at chinesemetasoft.com was **not** used: four of its yin/yang polarities
  (Gen, Xun, Si, Hai) contradicted the clean cardinal/corner pattern confirmed by Wikipedia and by
  our two verified flying-star charts. When sources conflict, the fixtures that reproduce published
  charts win. (This is the honesty frame in action.)
