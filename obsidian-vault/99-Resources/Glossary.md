# Glossary

Plain-language definitions, cross-linked to the primers where each idea is taught.

- **Bagua (八卦)** — the "eight symbols" map that divides a space into nine life areas. See
  [[../01-Feng-Shui-Fundamentals/The-Eight-Trigrams-and-Bagua|the primer]].
- **Ba Zhai (八宅)** — "Eight Mansions," the compass method that gives each *person* four good and
  four bad directions from their Kua. See [[../04-Week-3-Eight-Mansions/Eight-Mansions-Ba-Zhai|Week 3]].
- **Ba Zi (八字)** — "Four Pillars," a Chinese-astrology reading of a birth moment. We build only a
  taster (the year pillar). See [[../09-Bonus-BaZi/Module-Guide|the Bonus]].
- **BTB** — Black (Hat) Sect Tantric Buddhism, Professor Lin Yun's 1970s Western adaptation that
  aligns the bagua to the front door instead of the compass. See [[../07-Week-6-BTB-Contrast/BTB-and-Compass-Bagua|Week 6]].
- **Chi / Qi (氣)** — "life energy" or "vital breath"; the thing feng shui tries to keep flowing
  well. See [[../01-Feng-Shui-Fundamentals/Chi-Yin-Yang|the primer]].
- **Facing direction** — the compass bearing the front of a building "addresses." Genuinely hard to
  measure; competing definitions. Stored as `facing_degrees` in each home.
- **Flying Stars (Xuan Kong Fei Xing, 玄空飛星)** — the time-based compass school that gives a
  *building* a 9-palace natal chart. See [[../05-Week-4-Flying-Stars/Flying-Stars-Xuan-Kong|Week 4]].
- **Form School (Luan Tou, 巒頭)** — the oldest school; reads landscape and the shape of space.
  See [[../06-Week-5-Form-School/Form-School-and-the-Four-Animals|Week 5]].
- **Kua / Ming Gua number** — a number 1–9 from your birth year and gender that sorts you into the
  East or West group. See [[../04-Week-3-Eight-Mansions/Eight-Mansions-Ba-Zhai|Week 3]].
- **Li Chun (立春)** — "start of spring," ~Feb 4; the real start of the feng shui year. Births before
  it belong to the previous year.
- **Lo Shu (洛書)** — the 3×3 magic square whose "flight path" the Flying Stars follow.
- **Luopan (羅盤)** — the feng shui compass. We use two of its rings: the 8 directions and the 24
  mountains. See [[../01-Feng-Shui-Fundamentals/The-Luopan-and-24-Mountains|the primer]].
- **Mountain (24 Mountains)** — one of 24 named 15° compass slices, each tagged yin or yang.
- **Missing corner** — a bagua sector with no physical space in the home (an L-shaped plan).
- **Command position** — placing a bed/desk to see the door without sitting in its direct line.
- **Poison arrow (sha chi)** — chi rushing in a straight line at a spot (a corridor, a door aligned
  with a bed, an outside road or roof edge).
- **Trigram** — a three-line I Ching symbol (yang = solid, yin = broken); the eight trigrams sit at
  the eight bagua directions.
- **Wu Xing (五行)** — the Five Elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water) and their generating and
  controlling cycles. See [[../01-Feng-Shui-Fundamentals/The-Five-Elements-and-Cycles|the primer]].
- **Yin / Yang (陰陽)** — the two complementary qualities of chi (still/receptive vs. active/bright).
